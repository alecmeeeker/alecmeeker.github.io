Bushwick Daily Groundhog Day Benefit

Five bands and a DJ at Our Wicked Lady, for a suggested donation to the paper’s local reporting.

When

Where
Our Wicked Lady, 153 Morgan Avenue, Bushwick
Lineup
Razor Braids (headliner), New Rock City, Grandma, Eclectic Charango Beats, Josephine Network. DJ NiteOwl.
Door
$15 suggested donation, RSVP on DICE. Proceeds to Bushwick Daily.
