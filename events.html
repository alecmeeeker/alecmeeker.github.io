Events

Nights built from a venue, a lineup and a reason: five productions in Bushwick between 2021 and 2025, three of them raising money for the neighborhood’s newspaper.

Benefit nights for local journalism
  • 2025·10 The Armored League at 3 Dollar Bill Full-contact armored combat in the Yard, then a Halloween dance floor until 2 AM. Eight fighters, DJ Umfang and Anthony Romano, a vendor market and a costume contest, in a 1,250-capacity room. Producer, for The Armored League.
  • 2022·04 Bushwick Daily Rooftop Showcase Round two, on Our Wicked Lady’s roof: chrrrypop, Whaat, PowerSnap and SAFER, closed out by DJ Mari Mac Dowell. Members in free. Producer, for Bushwick Daily.
  • 2022·02 Bushwick Daily Groundhog Day Benefit Five bands and a DJ at Our Wicked Lady, Tanteo Tequila behind the bar, and the door going to the newsroom. Razor Braids headlined. Curator and producer, for Bushwick Daily.
  • 2021·12 Bushwick Daily Holiday Party A holiday party and year-end fundraiser at Three Diamond Door, with food from BK Jani and Blue Collar Burger for members. Presented by Bushwick Daily.
  • 2021·07 GREEN Two nights at DorDor Gallery on community answers to environmental problems: earth architecture, sustainable fashion, video art from the Venice Architecture Biennale, live music and talks with local leaders. Presented by Bushwick Daily, with DorDor Gallery.
