Live events, produced for Bushwick Daily and The Armored League.

Groundhog Day Benefit · Our Wicked Lady · February 2, 2022 The night in photographs

Photographs by Kyle Got Luck.

  • 2025·10 The Armored League at 3 Dollar Bill A full-contact armored combat tournament that became a Halloween dance party, in a 1,250-capacity Bushwick venue. More than 600 in attendance. Producer, for The Armored League.
  • 2022·04 Bushwick Daily Rooftop Showcase Round two of the benefit shows, on Our Wicked Lady’s roof: chrrrypop, Whaat, PowerSnap and SAFER, with DJ Mari Mac Dowell. Producer, for Bushwick Daily.
  • 2022·02 Bushwick Daily Groundhog Day Benefit Five bands and a DJ at Our Wicked Lady, for a suggested donation to the paper’s local reporting. Razor Braids headlined. Curator and producer, for Bushwick Daily.
  • 2021·12 Bushwick Daily Holiday Party A holiday party and end-of-year fundraiser for local journalism at Three Diamond Door, with food from BK Jani and Blue Collar Burger for members. Presented by Bushwick Daily.
  • 2021·07 GREEN Two days at DorDor Gallery on community solutions to environmental issues: earth architecture, sustainable fashion, video art, live music and talks with local leaders. Presented by Bushwick Daily, with DorDor Gallery.
