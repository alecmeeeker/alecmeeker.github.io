Commercial production for Bushwick Daily’s own products.

2020 Passport to Bushwick: the Beer Book spots Three commercials for the Brooklyn Beer Book, the companion to the Passport to Bushwick. One idea per spot: the toast, the first sip, the glass. Shot on saturated color backdrops in tight close-up, cut short and set to type for square and portrait social feeds. Producer. Edit: Sheila Maria Lobo. Finishing: Paul Del Gesso.
