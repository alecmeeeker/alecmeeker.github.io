The System

The Provenance-Grounded Research Agent

A production multi-agent research platform built for a working newsroom: multi-model orchestration routes Claude for generation, Haiku for classification, Gemini Flash for routing, and GPT-4 for specialized extraction. A PostgreSQL warehouse with pgvector and HNSW indexing fuses vector similarity with full-text search through Reciprocal Rank Fusion. An adaptive memory system with deterministic quality gates learns from human feedback. Every agent-initiated action clears human approval before it touches the paper.

It is deployed across news research, story extraction, and email triage, and it runs every day.
