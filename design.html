Design

Web, identity, print and editorial design, most of it for Bushwick Daily. Each piece carries its credits.

2026 Bushwick Daily, the website The paper’s site, rebuilt in three phases from February to August 2026 on its existing theme: the homepage, article pages, the phone layout and the membership pages. Design direction. bushwickdaily.com
2024–26 Bushwick Daily identity The square logo and wordmark studies of 2024, and the 2026 BD monogram, built from the wordmark’s own B and D. Design, 2024. Design direction, 2026.
2019–26 Passport to Bushwick Bushwick Daily’s annual drink passport for the neighborhood’s bars, sold to fund the paper. Editions in 2019, 2020, 2025 and 2026. Creative direction. Design: Matthew Perratore. passporttobushwick.com
2026 Bushwick Daily media kit The 2026 media kit and rate card for advertisers, published on the paper’s site. Design direction. bushwickdaily.com/media-kit
2026 The Bushwick Grocery Price Index Nine years of grocery receipts from the same 21-item basket, set as a data story with an interactive dashboard. Design direction. Reporting: YJC student journalists. The Historical Record
2026 Column carousel for Instagram A template for columns, reviews and essays: the writer’s own passages, set in white serif on black, with key phrases in yellow and red. Design direction.
