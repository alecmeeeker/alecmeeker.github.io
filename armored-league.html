Events · October 16, 2025

The Armored League at 3 Dollar Bill

An armored combat tournament that turned into a Halloween dance party, in a 1,250-capacity Bushwick venue.

When
Thursday, October 16, 2025. Doors at 6; the tournament from 7 to 10 in the Yard; DJ sets until 2.
Where
3 Dollar Bill, 260 Meserole Street, Bushwick
Program
Eight fighters in a single-elimination tournament under international Buhurt rules, then a costume contest and a vendor market.
Music
DJ Umfang and Anthony Romano
Audience
More than 600
Credits
Produced by Alec Meeker for The Armored League. Media partner: Bushwick Daily.
The night, from the audience’s own stories

Instagram stories reposted by the venue, the league, the fighters and the crowd, October 16 and 17, 2025. The clips belong to the people who posted them.

The production
Marketing
Positioned the night as a tournament and a Halloween dance party on one ticket, with Bushwick Daily as media partner.
Talent
Booked the eight fighters and negotiated their contracts.
Venue
Booked 3 Dollar Bill and negotiated the deal for the Yard and the main room.
Production
Led the setup and the day-of build-out; hired the first assistant director and the production assistants.
Safety
Booked the EMT and held the safety plan for full-contact bouts.
Show
Directed the live show: the bouts, then the costume contest and the sword raffle.
Market
Brought in the vendors, including the Bushwick collective Kaleidoscope Studios.
Music
Booked DJ Umfang, a co-founder of Discwoman, and Anthony Romano; hired the costume-contest judge.
Video
Managed the live video crew and the content collection.
Close
Ran teardown and the night’s settlement with the venue, fighters, DJs, vendors and crew.
